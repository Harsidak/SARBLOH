\section{Motivation: Perception as the Primary Bottleneck}
\label{sec:motivation}

Before designing the agent architecture, we conducted controlled empirical ablations to isolate where autoregressive reasoning models expend their exploratory action allocations during interactive problem solving.

\paragraph{Observation geometry dictates search complexity far more than reasoning depth.}
We evaluated an identical frontier reasoning model (Gemini 3.1 Pro) on the opening level of the official environment \code{ls20}, holding the prompt template, temperature, and environment state transitions strictly invariant while altering only the input observation representation (Figure~\ref{fig:gemini}). 
When provided with the environment serialized as a raw $64{\times}64$ integer matrix, the model expended between 200 and 300 actions repeatedly colliding with invisible topological boundaries, exhausting the permissible budget without reaching the goal state. 
Recovering reachability and spatial adjacency from 4,096 serialized integers imposes an associative retrieval burden that multi-head attention mechanisms execute unreliably \citep{rahmanzadehgervi2024blind}. 
Supplying a rendered raster image of identical board state reduced the required exploration by an order of magnitude to 12--24 actions. 
Augmenting this visual input with an explicit structural decomposition of terrain, HUD gauges, and color parity further consolidated the trajectory to 12 actions. 
Finally, enabling the model to express search routines as executable Python scripts within a sandboxed runtime cleared the level in 6 actions, achieving super-human efficiency relative to the human baseline of approximately 10 actions. 
Critically, over 90\% of the aggregate efficiency gain resulted from structuring the perceptual representation rather than extending inference-time chain-of-thought deliberation.

\paragraph{Planning failure is downstream of perceptual misinduction.}
To decouple strategic reasoning capacity from state representation errors, we evaluated the model on a complex \code{ls20} level under two informational regimes: visual screenshots alone, and screenshots accompanied by an ``oracle briefing'' explicitly detailing sprite roles, mechanics, and geometric dependencies. 
Without the briefing, the agent squandered its full action budget interacting with inert visual artifacts. 
With the briefing, the model formulated an optimal plan and cleared the level in 6 actions. 
The underlying planning mechanism was functionally capable from the outset, and its operational failure was induced entirely by erroneous state perception.

\paragraph{Sparse delta encodings induce perceptual myopia across distant spatial structures.}
While transmitting only modified grid coordinates minimizes prompt token consumption, it systematically masks non-local causal couplings. 
In ARC-AGI-3 environments, local actuation regularly triggers distant visual consequences, such as stepping onto an activation switch in \code{ls20} updating a status icon across the display. 
Under sparse delta streams, the agent fails to observe this distributed covariance and falsely infers that its action was non-functional. 
Consequently, preserving full-frame topological awareness remains mandatory.

\paragraph{Core knowledge priors distinguish mathematical certainty from inductive hypothesis.}
Because oracle briefings cannot exist for undisclosed evaluation environments, the agent must extract its own representations. 
ARC-AGI-3 is grounded in human core knowledge priors \citep{spelke2007core}, which are intrinsically geometric and topological. 
Properties such as lattice connectivity, bounding enclosures, midpoint tethers, and dihedral symmetries can be computed deterministically from grid coordinates without learned parameters. 
Conversely, operational affordances, such as agent embodiment and win conditions, remain latent until verified through physical interaction. 
Conflating these two epistemic categories causes models to treat transient guesses as ground truth, triggering catastrophic action loops.

\begin{tintbox}
\small\textbf{Summary of Empirical Motivations.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Perceptual Misinduction:} Attention mechanisms over serialized coordinate grids fail to infer topological adjacency, consuming hundreds of exploratory actions deducing baseline mechanics that are directly recoverable from visual rasters and computed geometric relations.
    \item \textbf{The Measured--Guessed Dichotomy:} Geometric and topological invariants hold unconditionally across unseen environments, whereas latent operational semantics require active, single-action hypothesis verification probes.
\end{enumerate}
\end{tintbox}
