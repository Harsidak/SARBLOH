Do not assume a button means what it means in other games. Map each available action once, on an open square, and write down what it did: a single step, a leap over the next cell, a slide that continues until something blocks it, a line that reaches across the board, or a click that needs coordinates. Some actions do nothing until a condition holds, so note the no-ops rather than discarding the action. The same action can behave differently beside a wall than in the open; test both. An unmapped action is the cheapest place to look for the mechanic a level is teaching.
