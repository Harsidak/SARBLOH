Some games move or copy things by a rule you can name: a shift by a fixed amount, a mirror across a line, a quarter turn, or a change of size. Tell them apart by acting once and comparing before and after cell by cell — a shift keeps the shape and changes position, a mirror keeps position and flips handedness, a turn changes both. Check what the rule applies to: the thing you control, a twin that copies your moves, or the whole board. Once you can name the rule you can predict any number of repeats without spending moves. Two mirrorings return the original, and four quarter turns do.
