Plan from what you know, not from hope. Before acting, say the goal you believe in, the shortest action sequence that reaches it under your verified rules, and what you expect to see after it. Batch actions only when every step is predicted by a verified rule. When a rule is still a guess, spend one or two actions that test it, choosing the action whose outcome tells your competing guesses apart. Count the cost: a level scores (human actions / your actions) squared, and a counter on the HUD is often the level's budget.
