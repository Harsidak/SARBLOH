Questions to ask about any game: What can I control, and what moves on its own? What does each action do, and does it depend on where I am? What stops movement (walls, edges, other objects)? What changes when two objects touch? What does the HUD count, and what happens when it runs out? Which object looks like a target (unique colour, a frame, a matching shape)? What changed at the last level-up, and what stayed the same? Early levels teach one mechanic; later levels combine the mechanics you already know.
