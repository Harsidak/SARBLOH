A world model is your best guess of the game's rule, written as Python: `step(state, move)` returns the next state. The game is deterministic, so a correct model predicts every step exactly. Build it from the change lines: which object moved, how far, what blocked it, what appeared or vanished, what the HUD did. Keep it small and concrete ("A1 moves obj 4 up 5 until a G wall"). You do not need proof to act on it: act on your best model and compare. When a prediction is wrong, the model is wrong there: fix it, mark the old rule refuted and say what really happened.
