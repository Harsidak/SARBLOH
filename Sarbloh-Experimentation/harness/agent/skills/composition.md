The first level teaches one mechanic and gives you room to experiment; later levels keep that mechanic and add another. When a level starts, first assume everything you established still holds, then look for the one thing that is new — an extra object, a second colour, a region you have not seen. Do not rebuild your model from nothing: find the rule that broke and fix only that. A prediction that fails on a kind of transition you have already seen means an old rule was too broad. A transition that no rule covers at all means something new has arrived. Those two need different responses.
