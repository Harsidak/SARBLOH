Some boards keep changing after your move. Watch whether the change advances one step at a time along a chain, spreads outward from a source filling whatever it can reach, or flows along a path you can switch. Tell them apart by waiting a turn: a chain advances one link, a spread grows on every open side at once, a flow stops when you close its route. You usually cannot outrun a spread, so act on its source or its boundary rather than chasing its front. Count how many turns remain before it reaches whatever you must protect, and treat that count as your budget.
