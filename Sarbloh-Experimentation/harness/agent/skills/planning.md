How to play a level. 1) Look (free): name what you might control, the walls, the targets and any counter on the edge. 2) Learn the dynamics: while you do not know what the controls do or what wins, send probe moves whose result tells your ideas apart; as soon as you know, stop probing. 3) Model it: write `step(state, move)` in `ipython` from what you saw. 4) Search and act: search the model (BFS) for the shortest path to your goal and send it with `play(path, predict=...)` in the same call. `play()` checks the board after each move and stops where it differs from the model; then fix the model and search again. 5) Later levels: start from your code, skills and lessons, and test only what is new. Every level can be solved: if search finds no path, test the rule you trust least. When two goals compete, spend one move that tells them apart.
