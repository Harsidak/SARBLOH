Plan from your model, not from hope. Before acting, say the goal you believe in, search your `step()` model for the shortest move sequence that reaches it, and say what you expect to see after it. Send the whole sequence in one `act()` and check the board against your model as it goes; when the game disagrees, stop, fix the model and search again. When two guesses compete, spend one or two moves on the move whose outcome tells them apart. Count the cost: a level scores (human actions / your actions) squared, and a counter on the HUD is often the level's budget.
