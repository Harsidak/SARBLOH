Levels often ask you to match one set of things to another: a colour to the same colour, a key to the door it opens, a shape to its outline, a thing to the slot it fits. Look for two groups of equal size before assuming there is a matching at all. Test one pair with the cheapest action you have and keep the result, because the rule usually holds for the rest. The matching may go by colour, shape, size or order, and a wrong guess looks right until the second pair fails — so confirm on a second pair before planning the whole level around it.
