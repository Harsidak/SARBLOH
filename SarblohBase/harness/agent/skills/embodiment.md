Before planning, settle what your moves actually move. One body is the common case, but a move may drive two bodies at once, bend a jointed body one part at a time, carry an object that follows until you drop it, or steer a cursor that is separate from anything that acts. Find out by making one move and listing every cell that changed. If two things moved together they are one control; if only one did, there is a second control still to find. Carrying something changes what blocks you, so test whether you still fit through a gap you passed through empty.
